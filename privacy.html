Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Passly (“the app”) creates identification photographs that follow official size and composition requirements. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: your photos never leave your device, and we do not collect personal information.

Photos

All photo analysis and processing — face detection, background separation, cropping, compliance measurements and export — is performed entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to a server, never transmitted over the network by the app, and never seen by us. Photos you export are saved to your own device storage and remain under your control.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics that transmit data to us. We do not collect or receive your name, email address, photos, location, or any other personal data.

Purchases

Photo credits are purchased through the App Store. Payments and transaction records are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details.

Purchase verification is managed through the RevenueCat SDK, acting on our behalf as a processor. RevenueCat processes an anonymous installation identifier and product identifiers to validate purchases. It does not receive your photos. RevenueCat’s privacy practices are described at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Permissions

The app requests access to your photo library and camera only so that you can choose or take a picture to convert. This access is used solely for the feature you invoke and nothing is uploaded.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
