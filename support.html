Official-size ID photos, checked on your device.

How to take a photo that passes

  • Stand in front of a plain, light wall in even daylight — no direct sun, no lamp behind you.
  • Have someone take the photo, or prop the phone at eye level. Selfie angle causes most rejections.
  • Look straight at the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed.
  • No glasses, no head covering unless worn for religious reasons.

Why is my photo flagged?

The app measures your photo against the official specification you selected and explains every failed check in plain words. Common fixes: move the camera further away (head too large), retake in softer light (shadows or glare), or pick the correct country and document type.

Will my photo be accepted?

The app checks measurements against published specifications, but requirements are set by each authority and can change. Always compare the final photo with the official example page of the office you are applying to before you submit.
